Collage crop helper

Pick a piece, then drag a box over the part of the picture the collage should show. Drag inside the box to move it, or drag its gold corner to resize it. The previews show the result in each tile shape. Each tile shape has its own crop: pick the tab, drag, press Copy, then paste into the field of the same name in that piece in /admin (Portfolio pieces) and save. Wide and tall tiles use the square crop when theirs is empty.
